- **Andalusite:** Forms under **low pressure and low-to-moderate temperature** conditions. You typically see it in contact metamorphism (where rocks are baked by magma at shallow depths).
    
- **Kyanite:** Forms under **high pressure** conditions. It can form at low, moderate, or high temperatures, but it always requires significant depth/pressure, making it a classic indicator of regional tectonic collisions.
    
- **Sillimanite:** Forms under **high temperature** conditions. While it can exist at various pressures (from low-pressure contact zones to high-pressure regional zones), it strictly requires a lot of heat—typically well above 500°C to 600°C.
